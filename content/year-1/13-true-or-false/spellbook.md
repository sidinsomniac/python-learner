# Booleans and comparisons

- `True` and `False` are booleans.
- Comparisons: `==  !=  <  >  <=  >=`. They give booleans.
- `=` stores; `==` asks.
- Chains: `11 <= age < 17`.
- `and` (both), `or` (at least one), `not` (flip). Order: `not`, `and`, `or`.
- Use brackets when mixing `and` and `or`.
- `"x" in text` - does x appear inside text?
