# Arithmetic

| Symbol | Meaning | `7 ? 2` |
|---|---|---|
| `+ - *` | add, subtract, multiply | 9, 5, 14 |
| `/` | divide (always a float) | 3.5 |
| `//` | whole number of times it fits | 3 |
| `%` | remainder | 1 |
| `**` | power | 49 |

- Order: brackets, then `**`, then `* / // %`, then `+ -`.
- `//` and `%` together split big units from small ones:
  `hours = minutes // 60`, `left = minutes % 60`.
- Wizarding money: 1 Galleon = 17 Sickles, 1 Sickle = 29 Knuts.
