# Revision II: numbers, types, formatting and slicing

Three mixed challenges. First, a warm-up for the brain:

```checkpoint
q: 'What does `f"{7 / 2:.1f}"` give?'
options: ["3", "3.5", "3.50"]
answer: 1
why: "`/` gives 3.5, and `.1f` shows exactly one decimal place."
```

```checkpoint
q: 'What does `"Wingardium"[-3:]` give?'
options: ["Win", "ium", "dium"]
answer: 1
why: "`[-3:]` means the last three characters."
```
