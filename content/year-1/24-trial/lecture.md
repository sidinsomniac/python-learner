# The Trial: Sorting Hat Reforged

The Sorting Hat asks **5 questions**. Each answer is a letter, and each
letter belongs to a house:

| Answer | House |
|---|---|
| `a` | Gryffindor |
| `b` | Hufflepuff |
| `c` | Ravenclaw |
| `d` | Slytherin |

You'll rebuild the ceremony in **four stages**. Each stage is a complete
spell of its own, and the last one puts everything together. Everything you
need is from this year: loops, lists, `if`/`elif`, string methods and
f-strings.

```checkpoint
q: The answers are b, a, a, b, c. Which houses are tied for the most answers?
options: ["Gryffindor and Hufflepuff", "Hufflepuff only", "Ravenclaw"]
answer: 0
why: a (Gryffindor) appears twice and b (Hufflepuff) appears twice.
```

**Tip:** plan each stage in pseudocode before you write any Parseltongue.
The Pensieve is your friend if something goes wrong.
